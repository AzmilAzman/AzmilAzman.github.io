Reflection & Learning Journey
Growth from Lab 1 to Project 2

Lab 1 — First Composable

Started from zero with Jetpack Compose, learning that UI is just a function of state. Writing my first composable felt unfamiliar — years of XML thinking had to be unlearned. But the simplicity of declaring what the UI should look like, rather than how to build it, quickly clicked.

Lab 2 & 3 — State & Polish

Understanding recomposition was the biggest mental shift. When state changes, Compose redraws — but only what needs to change. Material3 theming taught me how a consistent design system saves time and creates professional-looking apps with minimal effort.

Lab 4 & Project 1 — Architecture

Navigation Compose and shared ViewModels introduced me to proper app architecture. I learned why separating UI logic from business logic matters — not just academically, but practically when debugging state issues across multiple screens.

Lab 5 — Persistence

Room was the hardest lab. KSP configuration, Gradle compatibility with AGP 9.x, Flow integration — each step had its own challenge. Solving them built real confidence in working with Android's build system.

Project 2 — Full Stack Mobile

Bringing together Room, Firebase, Retrofit, Camera, and GPS in one app showed me what production Android development looks like. Each pillar connected to a real user need. The debugging sessions — from KSP version mismatches to NewsAPI free-tier restrictions — taught me that engineering is mostly problem-solving, not just writing code.

SDG 16 Impact

People's Voice is more than an assignment. SDG 16 calls for transparent, inclusive institutions. An app that lets any citizen submit structured feedback, attach evidence via photo and location, and have it synced to a community database is a genuine step toward that goal — even at a small scale.
